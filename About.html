Woods E.Edition
Broadcasting & Media Production
Woods E. Edition is a startup operation that was founded on December 23, 2022 by Drezden Woods and will open for business as soon as the funding necessary to begin operations has been obtained. Woods E. Edition believes in the power of storytelling through photography and fashion. Every moment carries meaning, and every image has the potential to become a lasting legacy. Founded with passion, vision, and creativity, the Company specializes in capturing life's milestones with authenticity and originality. Whether it is portraits, senior photos, maternity shoots, birthday celebrations, engagements, or modeling sessions, Woods E. Edition focuses on preserving the essence of every subject—every smile, every expression, every emotion.



About Me

Photographer & Entrepreneur

Hello, I'm Drezden, a certified photographer based in Atlanta, Georgia, and the proud founder of Woods E.Edition.

I've had the privilege of working with creators, entrepreneurs, and businesses to help elevate their brands through impactful, high-quality photography.
With years of self-taught experience, industry-recognized certifications, and a passion for visual storytelling,
I specialize in capturing high-end portraits and dynamic events that leave a lasting impression.

My mission is simple: to provide an exceptional photography experience
while creating timeless images that tell your story with authenticity and style.

Whether you're planning your next event, celebrating a special milestone,
or looking to strengthen your personal or business brand,
I look forward to bringing your vision to life and creating memories you'll cherish for years to come.
